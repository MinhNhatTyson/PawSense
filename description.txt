PawSense is a new mobile/app for Vetenarian and Pet lover users. This project would allow vetenarian to declare: 
 1. Common diseases, symptoms and cures. 
 2. All types of cats, their lookings, their unique characteristic, etc... 
 3. Cat foods, medicines, etc... 

For other users, they can use the app to upload the image of their cat -> Reveal their type/species. If the cat has sickness, they can describe the sickness and the app will tell them what disease based on that information, alongside with the cures and medicines needed. 

There are 2 main actors: Vetenarian and Customer. Vet will use window application, Customer will use mobile app.
